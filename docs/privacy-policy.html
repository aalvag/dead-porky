Dead Porky Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Dead Porky collects, uses, stores, and shares personal data when you use the Dead Porky mobile application and related services.

1. Overview

Dead Porky is a personal wellness application designed to help users track health, exercise, recovery, and day-to-day wellbeing. The app may combine data entered directly by the user with data the user authorizes from supported health and wearable platforms.

2. Data We May Collect

2.1 Account and profile data

  • Name, email address, authentication identifiers, and profile data.
  • Basic preferences needed to personalize the app experience.

2.2 User-provided wellness data

  • Exercise, nutrition, hydration, sleep, body metrics, and habits you manually enter.
  • Messages or context shared with in-app assistant features.

2.3 Authorized health platform and wearable data

If you choose to connect supported health platforms or wearables, Dead Porky may read only the data you explicitly authorize. Depending on platform permissions and your setup, this may include:

  • Step count and daily steps.
  • Sleep summary and sleep stage information.
  • Heart rate data, including average and resting heart rate.
  • Other wellness metrics you later authorize in the app.

For Huawei Health Service Kit testing, Dead Porky currently requests read-only access only to steps, sleep, and heart rate data.

2.4 Device and technical data

  • App version, device model, operating system version, and diagnostic logs.
  • Notification tokens and technical metadata needed to deliver app features.

3. How We Use Data

  • Provide personal dashboards, wellness summaries, and user-facing insights.
  • Display steps, sleep, and heart rate information inside the app.
  • Support wearable connectivity and authorized health-data sync.
  • Improve reliability, security, analytics, and product performance.
  • Support optional assistant features that generate wellness guidance.

4. Read-Only Health Data Access

Dead Porky does not write health data back to Huawei Health during the current Huawei Health Service Kit testing phase. The app only reads the user data explicitly authorized by the user and displays it to that same authenticated user inside the app.

5. Legal Basis and Consent

Where required, we rely on your consent to access health and wellness data from third-party platforms such as Huawei Health, Health Connect, Apple Health, and supported wearables. You can revoke these permissions through the corresponding platform settings at any time.

6. Sharing and Service Providers

We may use trusted infrastructure and service providers to operate the app, such as authentication, database, analytics, notification, crash reporting, and AI infrastructure providers. These providers are used only as needed to run the service and are not authorized to use your personal data for unrelated purposes.

We do not sell your personal data.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal data for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. If you request deletion of your account, we will delete or anonymize personal data as required by applicable law, subject to lawful retention obligations.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect personal data. However, no system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Choices and Rights

  • You may choose not to connect health platforms or wearables.
  • You may revoke platform permissions at any time.
  • You may request access, correction, or deletion by contacting us.

10. Children

Dead Porky is not intended for use by children without appropriate parental or guardian involvement where required by law.

11. International Transfers

Depending on the services used to operate the app, personal data may be processed in countries other than your own. When this occurs, we will use reasonable measures intended to protect the data in line with applicable law.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
